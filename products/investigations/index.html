Where Tracing Time Actually Goes

01

A Few Hops Out, the Graph Turns Into a Hairball

Busy addresses have hundreds of counterparties at every hop. Nobody can read that by hand.

How DART solves it

  • Keep only paths that hold up in time: funds arrive before they move on
  • Set a minimum amount and a node limit per hop to see the main flow first
  • Paths stop at exchanges and other known entities instead of expanding into noise

FAQ

Fund Tracing Questions

How many hops can DART trace?

Hop depth is configurable. For daily screening we suggest 3 inbound and 1 outbound; investigations can go deeper, with a minimum amount and a per-hop node limit to keep the graph readable.

Why were some paths excluded?

DART requires funds to arrive at each hop before they move on. A path that runs backwards in time means the money could not have come from that source, so it is excluded, and the result says so.

Does DART trace across chains?

Yes for USDC moved through Circle CCTP. The path continues on the destination chain.

How can a law enforcement agency get access?

Apply through the law enforcement partnership page. We verify the agency before opening investigator accounts and agree the terms of cooperation directly.

Are queries recorded?

Yes. Every query is saved as an immutable snapshot with its parameters and full result, available at any time.
