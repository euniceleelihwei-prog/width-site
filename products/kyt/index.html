KYT · Transaction screening

Where Did This Deposit Come from, and Where Is This Withdrawal Going?

Exchanges, payment companies and custodians need every deposit and withdrawal checked before funds are credited or released. DART traces the source and destination of one specific transfer, with separate rulesets for each direction, and returns a score, a verdict and the evidence behind it.

What KYT has to get right

Where Deposit and Withdrawal Screening Breaks Down

01

A Deposit May Carry Frozen or Stolen Funds

Once it is credited, a freeze or a recovery claim can land on you and your user.

How DART solves it

  • Deposits (inbound): trace the transfer upstream for sanctioned, issuer-frozen, hacked, scam or mixer funds
  • Only funds that arrived before they moved on are counted; backwards paths are dropped
  • Scored by share of risky funds, so you can credit, review or hold before the money lands

Proof300,000–400,000 API calls a day in production, across exchanges, wallets, custodians and card programs.

02

A User Withdraws to a Risky Address and You Carry the Risk

How DART solves it

  • Withdrawals (outbound): check the destination and what lies downstream: sanctions, terrorist financing, scams
  • KYT-IN and KYT-OUT rulesets are separate, so each direction gets its own policy
  • Or screen both sides in one call and get a merged result

ProofNew official actions ship as built-in rulesets: FinCEN's 9714 order was published on 6 November and the DART ruleset went live the same day.

03

One Transaction, Many Transfers

Contract calls and batch payouts often move tokens several times in a single transaction.

How DART solves it

  • List every token transfer in the transaction, in event order
  • Pick the one you care about with event_index (or by sender / recipient) and screen just that

ProofListing transfers is free. Only the transfer you screen is charged.

Where it fits

Where KYT Sits in Your Flow

  1. Deposit

    After confirmation

    Screen the source with KYT-IN. Clean deposits credit automatically; review and EDD cases go to a queue.

  2. Withdrawal

    After request

    Screen the destination with KYT-OUT and stop high-risk transfers before funds leave.

  3. Triage

    Four Verdicts

    0–19 pass, 20–49 review, 50–79 EDD, 80–100 block. Tune the bands to your risk appetite.

  4. Evidence

    Snapshot Saved

    Every screening's parameters and full result are stored as an immutable snapshot for audit.

Capabilities

What Comes with Every Screening

Direction-Aware

Deposits look upstream, withdrawals look downstream, each with its own rules and weights.

Time Order

A hop counts only if funds arrived before they left. Fewer false alarms.

Materiality Thresholds

Further hops need a bigger share: 5% / 10% / 15% / 20% at hops 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 by default.

Exchange Exemption

Paths stop at other exchanges, so their customers' history isn't pinned on yours. Can be switched off.

Cross-Chain

USDC moved through Circle CCTP is followed onto the destination chain.

Batch Rescans

Upload past transactions by CSV and re-screen them in one go, at launch or after a rule change.

FAQ

Transaction Screening Questions

Why use different rulesets for deposits and withdrawals?

They answer different questions. For a deposit you want to know whether the money is tainted; for a withdrawal, whether it is going somewhere it shouldn't. DART ships built-in KYT-IN and KYT-OUT rulesets that you can tune separately.

Will screening slow down deposits and payouts?

Sanctions checks return in under a second and most screenings finish within a minute. For very active addresses traced deep, use async mode and act when the result is ready.

What if a transaction contains several token transfers?

Call the transfer list endpoint to see every token transfer in the transaction, then pass the event_index of the one to screen. Listing transfers is not charged.

Can DART follow funds across chains?

Yes for USDC moved through Circle CCTP. The path continues on the destination chain.

We already run a KYT tool. Can we test DART first?

Yes. Keep your current tool running and send us a batch of past transactions. We return a result and risk path for each, with a diff against your current tool. DART is free until 31 December 2028.
