FAQ

Sanctions Screening Questions

Which lists does the sanctions database cover?

Official sources including OFAC, the UN, the EU and the UK, covering entities, individuals, companies and onchain addresses. It is updated continuously.

How soon can we use a new official action?

We turn new official actions into built-in rulesets. FinCEN's 9714 order, for example, was published on 6 November and was available in DART the same day.

How fast is sanctions screening?

Under a second, which makes it practical before transfers and during onboarding.

Can DART catch an address that isn't listed but deals with listed ones?

Yes. DART checks direct and multi-hop exposure in time order and by share of risky funds, and shows the full path. For sanctions use cases you can switch off the exchange exemption so paths are followed through exchanges too.

Can we run sanctions screening without full KYT?

Yes. Enable only the sanctions rulesets, or screen only the address's own labels, depending on what your compliance policy requires.
