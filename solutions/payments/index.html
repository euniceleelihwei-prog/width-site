Payments challenges

What PSPs and Card Issuers Deal With

01

One Bad Deposit Can Put a Whole Rail at Risk

Once tainted funds reach a card scheme or bank, partners start looking hard at your entire business.

How DART solves it

  • Screen the source before top-up or deposit: sanctions, issuer freezes, hacks and scams are handled by disposition level
  • Trace upstream in time order, counting only money that arrived before it left

ProofA Middle East card issuer screens every top-up source, so tainted funds never reach card schemes or fiat rails.

02

Block Everything or Let Everything Through

With only pass or reject, blocking too much loses customers and passing too much takes on risk.

How DART solves it

  • Four dispositions: 0–19 pass, 20–49 review, 50–79 EDD, 80–100 block
  • EDD asks the customer for source of funds instead of refusing outright
  • Thresholds and rule weights follow your own risk appetite

ProofDispositions and rule weights are documented in the product manual, ready to attach to internal policy.

03

Another Tool Flags Risk but Can't Say Why

A "medium-low risk" verdict with no reasoning leaves you unable to pass the payment or justify blocking it.

How DART solves it

  • A hop only counts if funds arrived before they left. Backwards paths are dropped
  • Results list path, hop, amount, share of funds and points

ProofIn one payment company's test, another tool rated an address "medium-low risk, needs review". DART scored it 0. Broken down, the risky funds could not have reached it in time.

FAQ

Questions Payments Companies Ask

Our bank partner wants zero tolerance. How do we configure DART?

Set sanctions, terrorist financing and similar categories to block and lower the score thresholds. Rulesets are saved separately, so you can show exactly which rules applied at any point in time.

What does EDD look like in practice?

Ask the customer for a source-of-funds explanation, then have compliance review it alongside the path, amount and share listed in the result, rather than refusing outright.

What can we hand an auditor?

Every screening has an immutable snapshot of the request and the full result. Pull them in bulk over the API to report on any period.

We work with several banks. Can rules differ per partner?

Yes. Copy a built-in ruleset per partner and pass the matching ruleset with each screening.

We only handle stablecoins. Is DART enough?

DART covers USDT and USDC on 11 chains. If your business is mostly stablecoins it can run on its own; if you also handle other assets, run it alongside your existing tools, each covering its own assets.
