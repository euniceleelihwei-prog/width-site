Architecture

Every Layer Is Our Own

Data, labels, rules and scoring are all built in-house, so they can follow your policy and every conclusion can be traced to its source.

  1. Layer 1

    Onchain Data

    USDT and USDC flows on 11 chains, updated in near real time.

  2. Layer 2

    Address Labels

    200M labeled addresses, over 90% of them exchange addresses, plus continuously updated sanctions, freeze and hack labels.

  3. Layer 3

    Time-Ordered Tracing

    Hop-by-hop expansion upstream and downstream, keeping only paths that work in time.

  4. Layer 4

    Rules and Scoring

    Path-matching rules flag violations; the scoring ruleset turns them into a 0–100 score and a disposition.

Disposition

Four Dispositions, Not Block-All

Every screening returns a 0–100 score and one of four dispositions, so your team can triage instead of reviewing everything. Thresholds and the points behind each rule are yours to tune.

  • A self-hit on a severe label such as sanctions scores 100 and blocks outright
  • Inbound and outbound exposure are weighted separately, and further hops weigh less
  • Every screening is kept as an immutable snapshot

FAQ

Common Questions

Which chains and tokens does DART cover?

DART focuses on USDT and USDC across 11 chains. Paste an address and DART finds every chain and token it has flow on, then screens them in one go. Keep your existing tools for BTC and other assets; each covers its own.

How many hops does it trace, and is it slow?

Hop depth is configurable; for everyday screening we suggest 3 hops inbound and 1 outbound. Sanctions screening returns in under a second. Most address screenings return within a minute; very active addresses traced deep take longer and can run in async mode.

Can we change the rules?

Yes. Built-in rulesets are read-only; clone one and change any condition, threshold or weight. Each ruleset is saved separately, so you can always show which rules were in force at a given time.

Can we show results to a regulator?

Every point maps to a rule, and each result lists the path, hop, amount, share and points. Every screening is saved as an immutable snapshot you can pull from the console or API.

What does it cost?

DART is free until 31 December 2028, with console, API and MCP included. See pricing.
