Why is it free?

We want more institutions screening in time order, and we want to sharpen the product on real business. During the free period you get the full product and engineering support, not a trial version.

No. Console, API and MCP are all included. Before you go live, we size capacity to your volumes.

Yes, and we recommend it. Keep your current tool running, send the same addresses to DART, and we'll return every result with an explanation of any differences.
